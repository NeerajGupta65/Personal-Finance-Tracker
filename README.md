# 🎓 Neeraj Expense Tracker - Student Edition

A personalized, expense-only personal finance tracker tailored for students. Track **Daily & Monthly Expenses**, manage **Pocket Money & Allowances**, prevent overspending with the **Daily Safe Spend Calculator**, and plan purchases with the **Student Wishlist**.

---

## 🌟 Student-Centric Features

- **💰 Pocket Money & Allowance Tracker**:
  - Set your monthly allowance/pocket money using the ✏️ button.
  - Live progress bar showing remaining balance and percentage spent.

- **🛡️ Daily Safe Spend Limit (Student Tool)**:
  - Automatically calculates `Remaining Pocket Money ÷ Remaining Days in Month`.
  - Tells you exactly how much you can spend per day to never run out of money before the month ends!

- **🍔 12 Tailored Student Categories**:
  - 🍔 Canteen, Mess & Food Delivery
  - ☕ Chai, Coffee & Hangouts
  - 📚 Books, Stationery & Printing
  - 🚌 Bus, Metro & Auto Travel
  - 🏠 Hostel / PG Rent & Mess
  - 📱 Mobile Recharge, Wifi & OTT
  - 🛍️ Shopping & Grooming
  - 🎓 College, Tuition & Exam Fees
  - 💻 Tech Gadgets & Accessories
  - 🎮 Gaming & Entertainment
  - 🏥 Health & Medical
  - 📦 Other Expenses

- **⚡ Quick Expense Logging & Tags**:
  - Quick amount chips (`+₹20`, `+₹50`, `+₹100`, `+₹200`, `+₹500`, `+₹1000`).
  - 1-click student tag chips (`#canteen`, `#chai`, `#books`, `#swiggy`, `#metro`, `#hostel`, `#recharge`).
  - 5-second **Undo** toast for accidental deletions.

- **📅 Month-Wise Navigation & Grouping**:
  - Step month-by-month with `◀` and `▶` buttons or dropdown.
  - View full history grouped by month with monthly debits subtotals.

- **🎯 Student Wishlist & Savings Target**:
  - Plan items you want to buy (Headphones, Trip with friends, Books, Laptop) and log savings towards them.

- **📊 Visual Charts & Analytics**:
  - Daily (14-day) and Monthly spending trends bar chart.
  - Category breakdown donut chart showing your top expense hotspots.

- **🌱 100% Fresh Start (Clean 0 Baseline)**:
  - Starts completely empty so you can input fresh data right away.
  - LocalStorage persistence (100% private, works offline).
  - One-click CSV and JSON export.
